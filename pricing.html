Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our licensing, renewals, and guarantee.

How does the 14-day 100% money-back guarantee work?
If Code Snippets Pro does not meet your expectations or solve your workflow needs within 14 days of your initial purchase, simply reach out to our support team and we will issue a full 100% refund — no questions asked.
What is the difference between Yearly, Monthly, and Lifetime licenses?
Lifetime plans require a single one-time payment and grant perpetual updates and cloud access with zero recurring fees. Yearly plans renew annually at a discounted rate, and Monthly plans offer flexible month-to-month freedom.
Can I upgrade or switch my website tier later?
Yes! You can easily upgrade from 1 website to 10, 100, or Unlimited websites directly from your account dashboard. You will only pay the prorated price difference.
Do my snippets continue working if my license expires?
Yes. All snippets already activated on your WordPress websites will continue executing safely. An active license is only required for automated updates, new AI prompts, and ongoing Cloud Sync.
